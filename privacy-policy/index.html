Privacy Policy for Lumio

Last updated: December 15, 2024

Lumio ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Lumio (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address - Required for account creation and authentication
  • Profile information - If you sign in with Google, we may collect your name and profile picture

1.2 Location Data

With your permission, we collect:

  • Precise location data - Used for geofencing features to trigger location-based reminders
  • Approximate location data - Used for context detection and improving reminder accuracy

Note: Location data is collected only when the App is in use and you have granted location permissions. You can revoke location permissions at any time through your device settings.

1.3 User Content

We store the content you create in the App:

  • Goals and tasks
  • Reminders and notifications
  • Location markers for geofencing
  • App preferences and settings

1.4 Device Information

We may collect:

  • Device model and operating system
  • App usage statistics
  • WiFi network information (for context detection)
  • Crash reports and error logs

1.5 Audio Data

With your permission, we may access your device's microphone for speech-to-text features. Audio data is processed locally and is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • Deliver context-aware reminders based on your location and preferences
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Send you notifications and reminders as requested
  • Analyze app usage to identify bugs and improve features

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information:

3.1 Firebase (Google)

  • Firebase Authentication - Handles user authentication and account management
  • Cloud Firestore - Stores your goals, tasks, and reminders securely
  • Firebase Analytics - Collects anonymous usage statistics to improve the App

Firebase's privacy practices: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.2 Google Maps

We use Google Maps API to display maps and provide location services.

Google Maps privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 OpenAI (Optional)

If you use AI-powered features, your anonymized data may be sent to OpenAI's API to generate contextual reminders. Personal information is anonymized before transmission.

OpenAI privacy policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using:

  • Cloud Firestore - Your user data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Local Storage - Some data is stored locally on your device for offline access

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your data - View all data associated with your account through the App
  • Delete your data - Delete your account and all associated data at any time through the App settings
  • Revoke permissions - Manage location, microphone, and notification permissions through your device settings
  • Opt-out of analytics - Disable analytics data collection (may affect app functionality)

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it by law.

7. Children's Privacy

Lumio is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
